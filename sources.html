Where the data comes from

OpenHousing NJ combines five public data sources. We use each one as published and do not edit the underlying numbers. Here is who produces each source, what we take from it, and how current it is.

Home values

Zillow Home Value Index
Produced by Zillow Research, Zillow Group

A smoothed, seasonally adjusted estimate of the typical home value in each Zillow area, for all single-family homes and condos in the middle price tier (the 33rd to 67th percentile). We average the areas inside each municipality.

Used for
Home value, price-to-income ratio, price growth, value chart
Coverage
Monthly, January 2000 to September 2026
Used in score
Yes (price burden and price growth)

Household income and population

American Community Survey
Produced by the U.S. Census Bureau

Median household income (table B19013) by county subdivision, from the 5-year ACS estimates. Municipal population comes from the 2020 Census.

Used for
Median household income, population
Coverage
ACS 2019–2023 (income, in 2023 dollars); 2020 Census (population)
Used in score
Yes (the denominator of the price-to-income ratio)
Census table B19013 →

Zoning and land use

NZLUD
National Zoning and Land Use Database, run by the Eviction Lab at Princeton University

A national database of local zoning rules, including the Zoning Restrictiveness Index (ZRI), minimum lot sizes, inclusionary zoning, accessory dwelling units, parking requirements, and the share of land zoned multifamily. NJ coverage is limited to 105 of 564 municipalities.

Used for
ZRI and the zoning details in each panel
Coverage
Local zoning surveyed 2019–2022
Used in score
Yes (zoning, where available)
Eviction Lab NZLUD →

Market activity

Redfin Data Center
Produced by Redfin Corporation

City-level home sales data: median sale price, days on market, sale-to-list ratio, share sold above list, inventory, and homes sold. Redfin places are matched to municipalities and averaged over each place's latest three months. Statewide figures in the NJ snapshot come from Redfin's state file.

Used for
Market activity section and NJ snapshot
Coverage
Towns: May–July 2026. State: September 2026
Used in score
No (context only)
Redfin metric definitions →

Municipal boundaries

The official municipal boundary shapes drawn on the map and the municipality codes used to join every dataset. Boundaries load live when you open the map.

Used for

Used in score
