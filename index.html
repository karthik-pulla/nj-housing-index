How it works

Three ingredients, one score

Every municipality is scored from 0 to 100, where higher means more affordable.

1

We gather the data

Home values from Zillow, household income from the Census, and zoning rules from Princeton's NZLUD.

2

Each town gets a score

Price burden, price growth, and zoning restrictiveness combine into one affordability index.

3

You explore and compare

Search any town, see its full breakdown, and test your own assumptions.

The score isn't fixed. Price burden, price growth, and zoning each have a live slider on the map. Dial any of them up or down and every town's score and rank recalculate instantly.
