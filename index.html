How it works

Three data points, one score

Every municipality is scored 0–100 — higher means more affordable — from three ingredients, weighted and combined.

We gather the data

Home values (Zillow), household income (Census), and zoning rules (NZLUD) for every NJ municipality.

Each town gets a score

Price burden, price growth, and zoning restrictiveness combine into one affordability index, 0 to 100.

You explore & compare

Search any town on the map, see its full breakdown, and even adjust the weighting to test your own assumptions.

The index isn't fixed. Price burden, price growth, and zoning restrictiveness each have a live slider on the map — dial any of them up or down and every town's score and rank recalculates instantly.
